## abandon
**释义**: v. 放弃，抛弃；遗弃

**例句**:
1. "The captain gave the order to abandon ship." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "abandon" entry*
2. "She abandoned her studies and went travelling for a year." — *Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English, "abandon" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> The captain gave the order to ______ ship when the fire spread beyond control.
> A. abolish　　B. abandon　　C. absorb　　D. abuse

**正确答案**: B
**解析**: abandon ship 为固定搭配，意为"弃船"。abolish（废除法律/制度）、absorb（吸收）、abuse（滥用/虐待）均不适用于"离开危险船只"的语境。

## abolish
**释义**: vt. 废除，废止（法律、制度、习俗）

**例句**:
1. "Slavery was abolished in the United States in 1865." — *Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, "abolish" entry*
2. "The government should abolish the tax on food." — *Collins COBUILD English Dictionary, "abolish" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> Slavery was ______ in the United States in 1865 with the ratification of the 13th Amendment.
> A. abandoned　　B. abolished　　C. absorbed　　D. accelerated

**正确答案**: B
**解析**: abolish 专门指"废除法律、制度或习俗"，与 slavery（奴隶制）搭配最恰当。abandon（放弃具体事物）不涉及制度层面的废除。absorb（吸收）和 accelerate（加速）均不符合句意。

## abnormal
**释义**: adj. 反常的，不正常的

**例句**:
1. "They thought his behaviour was abnormal." — *Merriam-Webster Dictionary, "abnormal" entry*
2. "There was an abnormal amount of rain this summer." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "abnormal" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> The doctor ordered more tests after noticing ______ levels of white blood cells in the patient.
> A. normal　　B. abnormal　　C. informal　　D. enormous

**正确答案**: B
**解析**: abnormal levels = 异常水平，指偏离正常范围的检测指标。normal（正常的）与句意相反，informal（非正式的）、enormous（巨大的）语义不匹配。

## abstract
**释义**: adj. 抽象的；理论的 n. 摘要

**例句**:
1. "Truth and beauty are abstract concepts." — *Collins COBUILD, "abstract" entry*
2. "He is better at handling abstract ideas than practical problems." — *Longman Dictionary, "abstract" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:

> A. concrete　　B. abstract　　C. absolute　　D. accurate

**正确答案**: B
**解析**: abstract concept = 抽象概念，与"difficult to define in concrete terms（难以用具体术语定义）"呼应。concrete（具体的）是反义词，absolute（绝对的）、accurate（精确的）不匹配。

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> The fire was not an accident — investigators concluded it was a ______ act of sabotage.
> A. delicate　　B. deliberate　　C. desperate　　D. dramatic

**正确答案**: B
**解析**: deliberate act = 蓄意行为，与"not an accident（并非意外）"对应。delicate（精致的）、desperate（绝望的）、dramatic（戏剧性的）均不表示"故意的"。

## demonstrate
**释义**: v. 证明；展示；示威

**例句**:
1. "The experiment demonstrates that sugar dissolves faster in hot water." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "demonstrate" entry*
2. "Thousands of people demonstrated against the war in the capital." — *Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, "demonstrate" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> The study clearly ______ that regular exercise can significantly reduce the risk of heart disease.
> A. demonstrates　　B. distributes　　C. dominates　　D. donates

**正确答案**: A
**解析**: demonstrate = 证明，指科学研究"证明"了运动对健康的益处。distribute（分配）、dominate（主导）、donate（捐赠）语义不匹配。

## distinguish
**释义**: v. 区分；辨别；使…杰出

**例句**:
1. "It is sometimes difficult to distinguish right from wrong." — *Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, "distinguish" entry*
2. "What distinguishes this restaurant from others is the quality of its service." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "distinguish" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> To be a good art critic, you need to be able to ______ genuine masterpieces from skilled imitations.
> A. distinguish　　B. distribute　　C. demonstrate　　D. eliminate

**正确答案**: A
**解析**: distinguish A from B = 区分A和B，指鉴赏能力。distribute（分配）、demonstrate（证明）、eliminate（消除）语义不匹配。

## domestic
**释义**: adj. 国内的；家庭的；驯养的

**例句**:
1. "The airline operates both domestic and international flights." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "domestic" entry*
2. "Domestic violence is a serious social issue that needs more attention." — *Longman Dictionary, "domestic" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> GDP growth slowed due to weak ______ demand, although exports remained strong.
> A. domestic　　B. dramatic　　C. dominant　　D. distant

**正确答案**: A
**解析**: domestic demand = 国内需求，与"exports（出口）"形成内外对比。dramatic（戏剧性的）、dominant（占主导的）、distant（遥远的）均不能与"exports"对照表示"国内市场"。
