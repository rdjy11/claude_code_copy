## eliminate
**释义**: vt. 消除；排除；淘汰

**例句**:
1. "The team was eliminated from the tournament in the first round." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "eliminate" entry*
2. "A move towards healthy eating could help eliminate heart disease." — *Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, "eliminate" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> The new screening system is designed to ______ candidates who lack basic communication skills.
> A. elevate　　B. eliminate　　C. embrace　　D. employ

**正确答案**: B
**解析**: eliminate = 淘汰/筛除，指筛选系统中"淘汰"不合格人选。elevate（提升）、embrace（拥抱/接纳）、employ（雇用）与筛选淘汰的语境相反或无关。

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> Unlike traditional industries, tech companies were quick to ______ remote working during the pandemic.
> A. eliminate　　B. embrace　　C. emerge　　D. employ

**正确答案**: B

## eventually
**释义**: adv. 最终；终于

**例句**:
1. "After years of effort, she eventually became a successful writer." — *Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, "eventually" entry*
2. "Don't worry — you'll get the hang of it eventually." — *Cambridge Dictionary, "eventually" entry*

**课后习题**（选词填空）:
> The negotiations were tough and lasted for months, but both sides ______ reached an agreement.
> A. eventually　　B. essentially　　C. extremely　　D. especially

**正确答案**: A
**解析**: eventually = 最终/终于，与"lasted for months"（持续数月）的时间线索相匹配。essentially（本质上）、extremely（极其）、especially（尤其）均不表示时间上的"最终结果"。
