### 2222 — Private ECU(s) or component(s) Serial Number data record
- 版本：v1 ｜ 验证方式：Test ｜ 适用：通用 ｜ Legacy ID：442733v0

To support read out of serial numbers for private ECUs or components that are required by legislation to have additional documentation and where the case is that the private ECUs / components does not support to be addressed directly by a tester. A data record with identifier 0xF13F shall be implemented only if the following conditions are fulfilled:• The ECU is a Public ECU master for Private ECU(s) or component(s) which has additional documentation requirements. The Public ECU master does not support gateway functionality for the private ECU(s) / component(s) in question, meaning that a tester can not read the serial number from the Private ECU(s) or component(s) by addressing it directly. The content of the data record shall only consist of serial number(s) for Private ECU(s) or component(s) that are subject to additional documentation requirements. The decision if a Private ECU or component will be subject for additional documentation is made by responsible authority at OEM. The content of the data record shall only consist of serial number(s) for those Private ECU(s) or component(s) that can not be directly addressed by a tester. The length of the serial number for a private ECU / component shall be maximum 12 digits. The coding of the serial number shall be alphanumeric. The serial number shall be right justified with any unused digits filled with 0, in total 12 bytes. If a private ECU / component, that is supposed to be present according to the ECU master, fails to answer when the public ECU master collect private ECUs / components part & serial numbers, this shall be shown by filling all bytes in the missing number with 0xFF. If the configuration of a vehicle excludes a private ECU / component (i. e certain private ECU / component are not mounted in the vehicle) this shall be shown by having all bytes in the serial number for that specific private ECU / component filled with 0x00. The content of the data record shall be the following: Byte Description#1 Total number of Private ECU(s) / component(s) Serial Numbers#2 -13 Private ECU / component #1 Serial Number#14 - 26 Private ECU / component #2 Serial Number.. ..#N - (N+12) Private ECU / component #X Serial Number

### 2236 — Customer setting parameters data record
- 版本：v2 ｜ 验证方式：Test ｜ 适用：通用 ｜ Legacy ID：344378v1

The purpose is to support the ECU replacement process at workshop Table "Requirements for definition of ECU/Vehicle status in data records" A data record with identifier 0xDED0 shall be implemented if the ECU stores customer setting parameter. The data record shall only consist of record data that contain information of customer settings stored by the ECU. The data record is used for restoring customer settings in the ECU replacement process at a workshop. A customer setting is defined as: A function that is open for alteration by the customer and/or tester tool it will be possible to change and adapt behavior of the functionality in the car to suit the customer. A long term alteration that only is initiated by the customer himself. The ECU is not allowed to change the customer setting parameter. Short term adjustments that, due to safety or other reasons, only are valid in one driving cycle or less and will revert to a predefined value are not to be considered as customer setting parameter. A setting that can be done by the customer himself and by the tester tool or only by a tester tool (at workshop or remote).

All tables in the sub-sections uses a unified format, including eight columns of information. The information in each of the columns, and general requirements applicable to all tables, are described below: Type of item: General description of the type of item that shall be monitored by the DTC test. Item no.: A sequential number for each separate item, used for references. Detected faults/errors: Definition of the fault, or error, that shall be possible to detect by the DTC test. The detailed criteria for increasing/decreasing the DTC fault detection counter (see section DTC fault detection counters) shall be specified by the implementer, unless it is specified in the table. Test PeriodIf a value is included in this column, the DTC test shall produce Test Samples, i. e. the DTC test fault detection logic shall execute and the DTC fault detection counter shall be updated (see section DTC fault detection counters for details regarding update of the DTC fault detection counter), periodically at this or a higher rate all the time while the Test Run Criteria are met (see column Test Run Criteria below) unless otherwise is specified by the table. The maximum period time between Test Samples shall be as specified by the table. However, in cases where the ECU application software uses a signal monitored by the DTC test more frequently than this specified maximum, then the period time shall be reduced to match that used by the application software. If no value is included in this column, the DTC test shall run one single time when the Test Run Criteria are met. Test Run Criteria: The Test Run Criteria defines the criteria that must be met for the DTC test to run. The Test Run Criteria can be defined both as Enable Criteria, e. g. "While output is activated", and as Inhibit Criteria, e. g. "Not during engine cranking". The DTC test shall run when all of the Enable Criteria are met and shall be inhibited if any of the Enable Criteria are not met, or if any of the Inhibit Criteria are met (Inhibit Criteria has priority over Enable Criteria, i. e. if at least one Inhibit Criteria is fulfilled while all of the Enable Criteria are met the DTC test shall be inhibited). If the test is inhibited it shall, as a minimum, stop updating the DTC Fault Detection Counter (FDC10, see section DTC fault detection counters). If the actual test can be stopped or not, or if it needs to run for other reasons, e. g. safety related or other application requirements, this shall be defined by the implementer. Note that if the actual test is not stopped and the ECU can take any action due to faults detected by the DTC test (e. g. reduce or shut off its functionality or request a warring indicator) that for sure can cause customer symptom the updating of the FDC10 should not be stopped (i. e. the test is not inhibited in this case). If the DTC test shall run one single time when the Test Run Criteria are met (no value is included in the "Test Period Time" column) the Test Run Criteria shall include an event description which will be a trigger condition for the test to run. The implementer shall specify if any partial DTC test results, obtained prior to the DTC test being inhibited by a Test Run Criteria, shall be used when the DTC test resumes execution. Note, however, that the value of the DTC Fault Detection Counter shall be maintained between the DTC test runs (see requirement specified in table "Requirements for the implementation DTC fault detection counters", number 10 found in section DTC fault detection counters). A robust strategy for measurement and filtering of the voltages shall be implemented. The actual method shall be designed by the implementer and shall comply with the following requirements. Vehicle battery voltage: The vehicle battery voltage Ubat shall be measured, by a dedicated ECU, as close to the actual battery terminals as possible (such that the measurement is unaffected by any internal energy storage in the measuring ECU). The Ubat shall be measured with a minimum accuracy of ±0.25 V and shall be sampled every 50 ms or faster. A new Ubat value shall be calculated every time a sample is taken. It shall be calculated as the average value of the samples taken during the last 500 ms period. The calculated Ubat values shall be sent periodically and received by all Public ECUs with maximum end-to-end time (including sensing, signaling and actuating time) of 100 ms. ECU supply voltage: The ECU supply voltage Uecu shall be measured with a minimum accuracy of ±0.25 V and shall be sampled every 50 ms or faster. A new Uecu value shall be calculated every time a sample is taken. It shall be calculated as the average value of the samples taken during the last 500 ms period. Low supply voltage fault detection shall only be done when all of the following conditions are fulfilled: (Ubat ≥ 12 V) AND (UsageMode == Driving) The calculated Uecu value shall be compared with the received Ubat value periodically each 500 ms: if Uecu < (Ubat - 3V) then the DTC fault detection counter (FDC10) shall be incremented by 3. if Uecu >= (Ubat - 3V) then the DTC fault detection counter (FDC10) shall be decremented by 6. The DTC shall have unconfirmedDTCLimit = 6. The DTC shall have confirmedDTCLimit = 10. The DTC shall have agedDTCLimit = 255. Examples of acceptable causes which may result in lost DTC information are listed below:1) Unexpected disconnection of the ECU power supply while the ECU is in operation, due to Vehicle battery removal, fuse blow, connector disconnection or other similar cause which does not occur as part of normal vehicle operation.2) ECU supply voltage below minimum specification due to e. g. weak battery or other vehicle related fault.3) Diagnostic service requests that results in an ECU reset.
